<h1>How to connect a printer to the company network</h1>

<h2>Overview</h2>
This article explains how employees can set up an email signature. <br> <br>

**Windows 11**
1. Open the **start menu**.
2. Search for **settings**.
3. Navigate to **Bluetooth & Devices**.
4. Select **Printers & Scanners**.
5. Select **Add Device**.
6. Add the appropriate printer to the device when it appears.
7. If the printer does not appear, select **Add a new device manually** and follow the prompts.

**MacOS**
1. Open System Settings.
2. Navigate to **Printers & Scanners**.
3. Select **Add Printer, Scanner, or Fax**.
4. Select the appropriate printer from the available devices.
5. When prompted, enter the appropriate information such as name, location and use.
6. Select **Add**.

**If the printer isn’t connecting to the device:**
* Restart your computer and try again.
* Make sure the computer is connected to the company network.
* Ensure the printer is powered on and connected to the company network.
* Ensure the correct printer is selected.
* If adding a printer manually, ensure the name and other information is correct.
* Contact the **IT Service Desk** if the problem continues.

**Applies to: Windows, MacOS**
